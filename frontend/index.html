The Act, as cited

Income-tax Act, 1961, as amended by the Finance Act, 2026

The 1961 Act was repealed from 1 April 2026 by the Income-tax Act, 2025. It still governs earlier years and pending proceedings; for income earned from 1 April 2026, check the 2025 Act, where section numbers and some rules differ.
